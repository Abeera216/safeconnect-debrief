Red flags in that email
📧
Fake sender address
The email came from a free Gmail account (recruit88@gmail.com). Legitimate employers always use an official company domain like @company.com.hk.
⏰
Artificial urgency
"Only 5 spots left" and "expires in 24 hours" are pressure tactics designed to stop you thinking carefully. Scammers don't want you to pause and verify.
💰
Unrealistically high pay
HK$180–220/hr for zero-experience, work-from-home tasks is far above market rate. If the offer sounds too good to be true, it almost always is.
🪪
Upfront personal information request
Asking for your student ID before any interview is a red flag. Scammers use this to steal your identity or target you with further scams.
👤
Vague sourcing
"We found your profile" with no specifics is a mass-blast tactic. Legitimate recruiters always explain clearly where they found you.

Stay safe in Hong Kong
💡 Golden rule: Always verify job offers through official CityU channels (the Student Affairs Office or SCOPE). Never pay a fee to get a job, and never share banking details with an unknown employer.
